Questions

Frequently Asked

◆
What breed are the puppies?

They are a Cocker Spaniel × Mini Goldendoodle cross. Their mother is a Cocker Spaniel and their father a Mini Goldendoodle (Golden Retriever × Poodle), so the puppies are part Cocker, part Poodle, and part Golden: a close cousin of the classic Cockapoo.

How large will they grow?

Every puppy is different, but given the parents we expect a small-to-medium adult dog. We are glad to share Chewy's and Rabbit's sizes when you get in touch.

Will they shed?

Poodle ancestry often means a lower-shedding coat, but with a mixed litter no one can honestly promise a non-shedding or hypoallergenic dog. If allergies are a concern, please spend time with the puppies before deciding.

When may they go home?

At eight weeks of age, in January 2027, after their veterinary examination and first vaccinations. We never send a puppy home any younger.

May we visit before deciding?

Certainly, and we encourage it. Get in touch and we will arrange a time for you to meet the puppies and both parents.

What is the price?

Please contact us for pricing and availability.
