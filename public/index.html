Questions

Frequently asked

What breed are the puppies?

They're a Cocker Spaniel × Mini Goldendoodle cross. Mom is a Cocker Spaniel and dad is a Mini Goldendoodle (Golden Retriever × Poodle), so the puppies are part Cocker, part Poodle, and part Golden, a close cousin of the classic Cockapoo.

How big will they get?

Every puppy is different, but based on the parents we expect a small-to-medium adult dog. We're happy to share Chewy's and Rabbit's sizes when you reach out.

Will they shed?

Poodle genes often mean a lower-shedding coat, but with a mixed litter no one can promise a non-shedding or hypoallergenic dog. If allergies are a concern, spend time with the puppies before deciding.

When can they go home?

At 8 weeks old, in January 2027, after their vet check and first shots. New York doesn't allow puppies to be sold younger than that, and we wouldn't want to anyway.

Can we visit before deciding?

Yes, and we encourage it. Get in touch and we'll set up a time to come meet the puppies and both parents.
